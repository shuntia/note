Try it

Wait: the bar leaves. Touch anywhere: it returns.

Tap the circle: the arc draws, the track appears beneath, the arc unwinds, and work begins on the most pressing task.

Swipe left: the next task. Swipe right past the first: work time on nothing in particular.

Tap the circle while working: pause. Tap again: back to it. Swipe down: done, and a bead joins the ring's opening.

Press D to jump to the last three seconds of the round or the break.

The hint under the circle shows the first three times only.
