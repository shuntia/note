Sundial

Proposals for Note, 2026-09-25. The principle: the arc is the control, form carries the information, words retire once learned.

1 · The circle

Idle, the arc breathes and one faint line sits under it. A tap starts work on the most pressing task; the queue becomes a strip you swipe through, with work time on nothing in particular one swipe the other way. Tap to pause, swipe down when done. No buttons. The bar rests when the hand does.

Start and finish

A subtle celebration on the arc itself, under a second. Pick from: ripple, ignite, breath for the start; close the ring, warm the ground, ripple pair for the finish. The flow above uses ignite + ripple to start and close the ring + warm to finish.

Form instead of words

  • Rounds are beads in the ring's opening. One per round today; each finish drops one in, so the day closes the circle. No "Round 2", no "52 min focused", and only one row of marks: the strip's dots appear only while a thumb is on the strip.
  • Urgency is a hairline. A rose line over an overdue task, a sun line over an urgent one. No badges.
  • A break runs the arc backwards in sage, and the ground dims a step. No "BREAK".
  • Steps are segments of the arc; done ones filled, the current one filling. No "2 of 3".
  • The next task peeks at the edge of the strip; the dots say where you are, a ring marks work time.
  • The hint retires. "tap the circle to start working" shows the first three times, then never. The same for "tap to pause · swipe down when done", once, inside the first session.
  • Time of day is the theme. Sky, below.
  • Desktop keeps the same face: click the circle, arrow keys or a horizontal wheel move the strip, Escape pauses, Enter finishes. The top bar already rests on idle; the face's hint is the only text added.

2 · Sky theme

A fourth choice next to System, Light and Dark. The sun's altitude, once a minute, blends four keyframes: day, golden, dusk, night. Ground colours cross-fade; text switches sets where the ground crosses the middle. The place comes from the time zone, or the device's location if offered.

3 · Time zone follows the device

A switch under the time zone row, on by default. When the device's zone differs from the saved one, the client saves it and shows a toast with Undo, which turns the switch off.

4 · List motion at any size

Three changes, none visible when the list is short.

  • Entrance as a wave, not a queue. Rows delay by their position on the page, the whole wave lasts a quarter second whatever the count, and rows below the fold are placed without animating. In two columns both columns rise together.
  • Bursts coalesce. A run of changed frames within 80 ms triggers one reload, each reload carries a sequence number so a slow older response cannot overwrite a newer one, and one FLIP pass runs from the positions before the burst.
  • No per-frame measuring. While rows fold away the list stops measuring every row on every frame; it measures once when the fold begins and once when it ends.

5 · No zoom on the phone

iOS zooms into any focused field whose text is under 16 px. The chat composer and the jot field sit at 0.92 rem, which is 14.7 px on a phone. On coarse pointers every text field goes to 1 rem, the viewport meta gains interactive-widget=resizes-content so the keyboard pushes the composer up instead of covering it, and buttons get touch-action: manipulation so a fast double tap never zooms. Nothing is mocked because nothing looks different.

Not in these boards

  • Light mode exists already; Sky reuses it as its day keyframe.
  • Rows removed by another device still vanish without a fold.
