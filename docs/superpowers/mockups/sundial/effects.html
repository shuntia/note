Focus on Reading 2.2
Letter 6.16.13–22

Start and finish

Each plays once, under a second, on the arc itself. Nothing falls from the sky.

Session start

Draw and unwind: from nothing, the arc draws clockwise, the grey track appears beneath, and the arc unwinds from its tail until only the track is left. Ripple: one thin ring leaves the arc. Ignite: a flare where the arc begins. Breath: the face draws in from slightly small.

Task finish

Close the ring: the fill runs to the end and a bead drops into the opening. Gradient ripple: a soft band of the arc's own colour widens out from the ring. Warm: the ground brightens for a moment. Pair: two thin rings a beat apart.

Round done to break

The ring closes in sun and a bead drops; the arc then tints to sage while the ground dims a step and the counter flips to 05:00; the break drains from the far end. At its end the ground brightens and the empty sun arc is ready.

Rounds

Beads sit in the ring's opening, one per round today; each finish adds one. This replaces both dot rows.
