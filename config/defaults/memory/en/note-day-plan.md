summary: Note day plan: the Today tab, nightly planning, the morning debrief, filling free time, and closing the day
---

Today tab: the circle shows what is next; swipe up or scroll to see the whole day as a line, the morning debrief, and the calendar.

Nightly planning: each night (03:00 by default) Note builds tomorrow from the user's routines, moves or drops blocks, adds urgent work, lays check-ins, rechecks goals, and writes a short morning debrief, shown at the foot of Today. Settings → Day → "Nightly plan" turns it on or off and sets the time.

Putting tasks into the day: "Fill my free time" (on the calendar section, or ask in Chat) lays open tasks into time marked Free, Now tasks first, then urgency and due date. "Schedule these for tomorrow morning" places specific tasks. A block can be started, pushed later, dropped for today, or moved to tomorrow.

Closing the day: at the close-the-day time (Settings → Day → "Close the day", 21:30 by default) Note asks what is still open and offers to carry it to tomorrow. Clearing the time turns it off.

Settings → Day → "Routines and blocks" picks which routines ping.
