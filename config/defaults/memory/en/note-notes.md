summary: Note's working notes and the Notes tab: what Note keeps in mind for the user, the Soon list and Later
---

Things to keep in mind: say "keep in mind that…" or "remind me to call the bank back" and Note writes it as a working note — one short line that every conversation and call reads, sometimes tied to a stretch of time ("until Friday"). Note brings it up when it fits, may nudge about it when the user is idle, and clears it once it is handled. Each night, notes that have run past their time or gone three days untouched are settled: kept as long-term memory when they still matter, or let go. Anything that needs a time or real work becomes a task instead.

The Notes tab is the task list: Now at the top, then Soon (urgent, or due or scheduled within 7 days), then Later folded away, Done today, and Goals. The field at the top adds a task or searches what is there, and a category filter narrows the list.
