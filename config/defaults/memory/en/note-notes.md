summary: Note notes and the Notes tab: things Note keeps in mind for the user, the Soon list and Later
---

Things to keep in mind: say "keep in mind that…" or "remind me to call the bank back" and Note holds it as a note, its own scratchpad that every conversation reads. Note brings it up when it fits, may nudge about it when the user is idle, and checks it off once the user says it is handled. Anything that needs a time or real work becomes a task instead.

The Notes tab is the task list: Now at the top, then Soon (urgent, or due or scheduled within 7 days), then Later folded away, Done today, and Goals. The field at the top adds a task or searches what is there, and a category filter narrows the list.
