category: procedural
summary: Note work sessions and pomodoro: how to start a focused session on a task, take breaks, and finish
---

Starting: on the Today tab, tap the circle ("Start working"), press Start on a block, or Start in a task's row menu in the Notes tab. Starting one ends any other running session.

While it runs: the circle becomes a gauge of the time left. Tap to pause, take a break, mark "Done with this step" for tasks with steps, or swipe down when done. Note checks in by itself partway through and near the end, and afterwards asks how the round went. Time worked is counted on the task.

Pomodoro: Settings → Sessions → "Pomodoro" turns on rounds (25 minutes of work and 5 of break by default; both adjustable). Note says when each round and break is up.

Also under Settings → Sessions: notify when a session ends, and show remaining or elapsed time.
