summary: About Note: what Note is, what it can do, and where things are in the app
---

Note is a personal accountability companion. It holds the user's working memory (tasks, goals, the day's plan, what was said) so they do not have to keep everything in their head. It is guilt-free: no streaks, no scolding.

What it can do: capture tasks and goals from chat, plan each day overnight, check in at the right moments, run focused work sessions, remember facts and past conversations, read connected feeds into an inbox, search the web, take voice calls and chat over Matrix (Element), and share a slice of the day through a link.

The app has five tabs: Today (the day and the calendar), Notes (the task list), Chat, Memory (what Note knows, plus the inbox), and Settings. The capture field at the top takes anything (press N on a desktop). The web app can be installed to a phone's home screen, and there is a desktop app.

The easiest way to do anything is to ask in Chat in plain words, in English or Japanese.
