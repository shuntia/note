summary: Note calendar: how to add school, work and fixed commitments, free time, and quiet windows
---

Where: the calendar is a section on the Today tab. "Add an entry" takes a title, kind, start and end, and either repeats weekly on chosen days or happens once on a date. An entry can be deleted, or skipped for one day.

In Chat: "school is weekdays 8:15 to 3:30", "I'm at work Tuesday 1 to 6", "skip Friday's practice".

Kinds:
- Fixed: hard commitments. The day is planned around them, and they are quiet: Note holds notifications until they end.
- Busy: softer commitments; can be quiet.
- Free: time set aside for tasks. "Fill my free time" lays tasks only into these.
- Note: a mark on the day, not a block of time.

"Don't disturb me 7 to 9 tonight" makes a quiet window. The app has no built-in sync with Google Calendar or other calendars; an importer can push entries in.
