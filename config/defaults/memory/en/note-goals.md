summary: Note goals: how to set a goal for something that runs for weeks and track it
---

A goal is something that runs for weeks: an application, an exam, a project, a move.

How: say it in Chat ("I have the entrance exam on March 3") and Note opens a goal, breaks it into 3 to 12 tasks with due dates spread back from the goal's date and a size each, and lays the near ones onto the plan. Or in the Notes tab, use "More ways to add" → "New goal…".

Goals show in the Goals group of the Notes tab with their progress; each can be renamed, given a date, or marked done or dropped. Ask "how is the exam goal going?" and Note says what is left and what it will take. The nightly plan rechecks goals against their dates and adds tasks to any that fall short.
